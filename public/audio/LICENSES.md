## `ambience-nature.ogg` / `ambience-nature.mp3`

A 20-second loop mixed for this map (birds, crickets, light breeze), at low
volume under the master mute. Every source is CC0 1.0 (public domain):

## `barks/`

Movement voice barks. The manifest lists the phrases; Adonay will record the
files. Until those files exist the game stays silent.
